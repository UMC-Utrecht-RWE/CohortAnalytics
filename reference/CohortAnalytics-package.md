# CohortAnalytics: Calculation or Model Estimates of Cohort Analytics

Common functions to compute effect estimates in (matched, weighted)
cohorts, such as incidence rates, prevalences, cumulative incidence
(1-KM) risks, risk ratios and differences, and hazard ratios.
